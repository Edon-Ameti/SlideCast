3 · Voice

A whole slide lets Kokoro carry phrasing across sentences, which is how it sounds when you paste a paragraph into its own UI. One sentence at a time makes every sentence start cold, but pins each sentence boundary exactly without needing alignment.

4 · Music

Judge the music against the real voice before committing to a build. The voice is generated once and reused, so getting the level right costs you nothing extra.

✓

5 · Subtitles

Lines break at commas and full stops rather than on character count, and none stays on screen for less than the shortest-line setting. Highlighting listens back to the generated voice to find each word, which adds about half a minute to the first build.

6 · Pauses

Every slide is held for exactly as long as its own narration, so there is no slide length to set. These are only the silences around the speech.

The pause between sentences is pure pacing and nothing happens in it. The pause between slides is where the fade to black lives, so the two settings are linked.

7 · Black fades

The video fades in from black at the very start, fades out to black at the very end, and fades out then back in between every pair of slides.
